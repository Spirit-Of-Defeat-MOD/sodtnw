Classes in china, unsure howd they come into play but noted them anyway


Bureaucratic / Comprador Bourgeoisie (官僚/买办资产阶级) - Top-tier financial elite tied directly to state power and foreign imperialist capital.
National Bourgeoisie (民族资产阶级) - Middle-tier domestic factory owners, regional merchants, and independent capitalists
Warlord Military Factions (军阀) - Regional military commanders holding autonomous control over provinces
Landlords & Gentry (地主阶级) - Rural elites owning large swaths of arable land. They did not engage in physical labor
Urban Petty Bourgeoisie (城市小资产阶级) - Intellectuals, university students, low-level clerks, craftsmen, and small shopkeepers. Highly literate but plagued by economic instabilit
Urban Proletariat (工人阶级) - Industrial factory workers, miners, and transit labor concentrated in hubs like Shanghai and Wuhan
Peasantry (农民阶级) - The overwhelming demographic majority. They either owned no land (tenants) or small plots

"stratum or strata" (阶层 - jiēcéng)
